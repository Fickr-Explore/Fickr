Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my In-App Purchases?

If you reinstall the app or switch devices, go to the Settings or Coin Boutique and tap the 'Restore Purchases' button. This will re-sync your balance linked to your Apple ID.

Where are my exported Moodboards saved?

Exported cards from the Moodboard Lab are saved directly to your device's Photo Library. Ensure you have granted "Photos" permission to the app.

How to re-take the Aesthetic IQ Test?

Your persona evolves. You can re-initiate the diagnostic test any time from the Profile Section to get fresh muse recommendations.

Issues with Color Story generation?

If the algorithm fails to extract a palette, ensure your image is in a standard format (JPG/PNG). Clearing the app cache in Settings can also help resolve processing glitches.
