Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Fickr - Aesthetic Lifestyle & Muse Insights. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data within the application.

2. Data Collection

Fickr is designed as a standalone visual experience.
• We DO NOT collect any personal information (PII) such as your name, email, or physical address.
• We DO NOT require an account or login from you.
• We DO NOT track your location.

3. Locally Stored Data

Certain information is only stored locally on your device for functionality:
• Your "Favorites" list: Saved within your device's persistent storage.
• Privacy Agreement status: To ensure you have consented to these terms.
This data never leaves your device and is not shared with us or any third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to external web content for aesthetic inspiration. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party websites.

5. Apple Guidelines Compliance

We comply with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines regarding User Privacy. This app does not use any tracking pixels or advertising identifiers (IDFA).

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not intentionally collect or maintain information from those we know are under 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via the Feedback section in the app.
